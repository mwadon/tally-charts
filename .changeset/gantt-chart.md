New `GanttChart`: a schedule against its plan, one row per task or milestone in data order, drawn on a time axis. Give `x` (name), `start` and `end`; a row without an `end`, or with an `end` equal to its `start`, is a milestone, drawn as a diamond (filled when reached, empty when not). `end` is an exact instant, not a day: a bar runs from `start` to `end`, and a date-only string is UTC midnight, so a task that should cover the whole of March 6 needs an `end` of March 7. `group` draws the rows of one group together under a heading (groups in the order of their first row, rows without a group first and without a heading). `now` decides what is done, and draws a today line with its date when it is inside the time axis: a task that ends at or before `now` is solid, one that starts at or after it is hatched, and a running one is solid up to `now` and hatched after it; the chart never reads the clock. `progress` (0 to 1 per row) overrides `now` for that row. `baselineStart` and `baselineEnd` draw a thin baseline under the bar (a small hollow diamond under a milestone, which reads only `baselineStart`); the part of a task's bar that runs past the baseline's length is drawn in the loss tone, and a milestone later than its baseline shows "+3d" in the same tone. A task that only started late has no overrun. Each row gets a value label where it fits (`valueLabels`, default true): the duration in whole days ("5d", "<1d"), the done share of a running task ("50%"), "+2d" for an overrun, and a milestone's date with its slip of at least one day ("+3d" late, "−2d" early). The chart draws a key above the plot (`legend`, default true) with the kinds the data has (done, planned, overrun, baseline, milestone), shown when at least two kinds occur. Names sit left of the bars when the chart is at least 480 px wide and above them in a narrower one (`labelPosition`: `'auto'`, `'left'` or `'top'`); long names are cut with an ellipsis and keep the full text in a `<title>`. `domain` sets the time axis (otherwise it covers all the dates, baselines included, rounded out to whole calendar steps, so in a narrow chart its end can fall between two drawn ticks); `zone` is `'utc'` or `'local'` (default `'utc'`); `tone` sets the bar tone (default `'accent'`). The height follows the content, so there is no `height` prop. Rows that cannot be drawn are skipped with a development warning: a missing or invalid `start`, an invalid `end`, or an `end` before the `start`; skipped rows are not points, but keep their name, with empty cells, in the data table. A task's baseline that is invalid, half missing or reversed, or a milestone's invalid `baselineStart`, is ignored for that row (a milestone reads only `baselineStart`, so a `baselineEnd` on it is ignored without a warning), a `progress` outside 0 to 1 is clamped (and one that is not a number is ignored), and an invalid `now` or `domain` is ignored, each with a development warning. Each drawn row is a `ChartPoint` whose id is the `id` accessor or the data index; there is a tooltip, keyboard navigation along the rows and a hidden data table. 22 new `ChartMessages` keys set the texts: `today`, `keyDone`, `keyPlanned`, `keyOverrun`, `keyBaseline`, `keyMilestone`, `statusDone`, `statusInProgress`, `statusPlanned`, `milestone`, `statusReached`, `statusUpcoming`, `percentDone`, `overBaseline`, `late`, `early`, `baselineRange`, `headerStart`, `headerEnd`, `headerBaselineStart`, `headerBaselineEnd` and `headerProgress`. Day counts are written by the locale (`Intl` unit formatting), so they need no plural forms.

```tsx
<GanttChart
  title="Kitchen remodel"
  data={tasks}
  x="name"
  start="start"
  end="end"
  group="phase"
  baselineStart="plannedStart"
  baselineEnd="plannedEnd"
  now="2026-03-27"
/>
```
